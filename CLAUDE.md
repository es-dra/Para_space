## Operating Mode

- Work in explicit research nodes with a frozen question, metric, stop rule, and
  artifact location before running experiments.
- Keep the first node small enough for the project owner to review.
- Do not open an unbounded research pipeline.
- Do not introduce external checkpoints, equivariant variants, update
  prediction, or training-acceleration claims unless a new node contract
  explicitly authorizes them.
